How to use Openseat

Find a day with reward seats, then book it on the airline site with your own miles. Below are four real ways people search, step by step.

The basics in four moves

Start with your miles Pick the program you hold miles in. Skywards, Etihad Guest or Privilege Club.
Finish the sentence Set cabin, travellers, airports and an optional return. Search by city, code or Arabic name.
Read the numbers Each day shows how many seats are open on its best flight: 1, 2, 3 or 4+. Empty days have none.
Book with the airline Open the day, follow How to book, and finish on the airline site before the seat goes.

Examples

A family of four to London for the winter break

Using Emirates Skywards, show me Economy seats for 4 travellers from Dubai to London, and back 2 weeks later.

Try this search
  1. 1With 4 travellers, a day only shows seats when one flight has 4 open. Look for days marked 4+ in dark green.
  2. 2Pick an outbound day, then switch to the Return tab. Days with a short bar fit a trip of 11 to 17 nights.
  3. 3Book both legs in one session on emirates.com, so the seats you found are still there for the second leg.

A last-minute Business seat to Bangkok

Using Etihad Guest, show me Business seats for 1 traveller from Abu Dhabi to Bangkok, one way.

Try this search
  1. 1Airlines often release unsold seats in the last two weeks, so check the first rows of the calendar.
  2. 2Use the arrows next to the date to step through days without going back to the calendar.
  3. 3If Business is full but Also open shows Economy, tap it to see that price on the same flight.

Two First seats to Tokyo with Avios

Using Qatar Airways Privilege Club, show me First seats for 2 travellers from Doha to Tokyo, one way.

Try this search
  1. 1Qatar sells First only on its A380, so many routes show not offered. That is a fact about the plane, not a lack of seats.
  2. 2Two seats together in First are rare. If the calendar is empty, tap Alert me when seats open.
  3. 3Short on Avios? You can move Avios from British Airways Executive Club before you book.

Starting from a city that is not a hub

Using Emirates Skywards, show me Business seats for 1 traveller from Riyadh to London, one way.

Try this search
  1. 1Flights from Riyadh connect in Dubai. A day shows seats only when both flights have one open.
  2. 2Each flight card shows the stop and the time on the ground, so you can skip long connections.
  3. 3Compare with a search from Dubai. If you can reach the hub yourself, there are often more days to choose from.

Common questions

Why did a seat disappear when I tried to book?

Availability refreshes at midnight and noon Gulf time. Seats can be taken between refreshes, so the airline site always has the final word.

Why can't I pick dates further out?

The window is always the next 90 days. That keeps searches fast, and a shared link shows everyone the same dates.

Are the prices exact?

Miles are the published reward price for one way, per traveller. Taxes are an estimate in US dollars and can change at checkout.

How do I share a search?

Tap Copy link next to the date. The link keeps your program, cabin, travellers, airports and return, so others see what you see.
